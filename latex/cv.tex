\section*{Recent Research and Projects}

\subsection*{Printable 3D Generation and Assembly Design for Complex Models}
\par\smallskip
2026.10–Now
Investigating part decomposition, interlocking connector design, and 3D printing for complex 3D models. The workflow connects generation or reconstruction, connector design, simulation, and physical assembly. The focus is on assembly feasibility, ease of assembly, connection reliability, and stability.
