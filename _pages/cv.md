## Recent Research and Projects

### Printable 3D Generation and Assembly Design for Complex Models

**2026.10–Now**<br>
Investigating part decomposition, interlocking connector design, and 3D printing for complex 3D models. The workflow connects generation or reconstruction, connector design, simulation, and physical assembly. The focus is on assembly feasibility, ease of assembly, connection reliability, and stability.

### CAI-WM: World-Action Modeling

**2026.06–2026.09**<br>
Contributed evaluation management, experimental result recording, and training-data production. In the September 18, 2026 snapshot of WorldArena 2.0 Track 1, the team model scored 70.07 overall, only 2.26 points behind the leader. It placed 5th in Trajectory Accuracy, 6th in Physics Adherence, and 9th in Aesthetic Quality among 81 models.<br>
[Leaderboard snapshot](https://huggingface.co/spaces/WorldArena/WorldArena2.0/tree/7f1bc898ab88a950da1aabb9d08f235bd2e743cc)

### Privacy–Utility Trade-offs in Life-Logging Video (RayNeo Period)

**2026.03–2026.04**<br>
Co-authored *Position: Life-Logging Video Streams Make the Privacy–Utility Trade-off Inevitable*, addressing privacy–utility trade-offs in persistent sensing and egocentric video systems. Second author, affiliated with RayNeo.AI in the manuscript; submitted to the NeurIPS 2026 Position Paper Track.<br>
